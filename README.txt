Church Music Library sheet selector fix

Copy the four files in this ZIP into the matching locations of your existing church-music-library project, replacing those files. Your music data, importer, player folder, and R2 settings are not included or changed.

On the song page, tap Sheets in the bottom bar to switch between PDFs. In Practice, tap Sheets in the audio bar to switch PDFs without stopping the audio. The previous top sheet tabs are hidden. The Sheets buttons appear only when the song has more than one PDF.

The service worker cache version changed so installed PWAs fetch the updated app files after deployment. If an iPhone keeps an older screen, close and reopen the app or refresh the page.
